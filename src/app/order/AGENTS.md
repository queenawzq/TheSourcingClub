### One balance, one card (Nov 20, 2026)

Every factory has the same Earnings card, however it withdraws or with no method yet: "Available balance (USD)" ("(estimated USD)" only when money in its own Stripe account was converted), its number the server's Available. The hand-paid "Balance (USD)" card and its help are gone; do not bring a second version back.

- The four figures and the card come from the server only, one read for owner and member alike: `/api/stripe-connect` `figures` (`readBalance`: `factory_balance(org)` with the company's Stripe money added, `reach`, the PayPal/Wise withdrawals), asked by `useFactoryBalance` on load and when money moves (`balance-reader.mjs`); `factory_balance(org, client)` for the client filter. Never merge two reads in the browser. Total earnings is the server's figures added up (`summaryFrom`: the four plus `processing_cents`), never worked out on its own. Never sum payments into a figure in the browser.
- If the balance call answers 502 with `figures` (Stripe unreachable), show those figures; never blank the card.
- Withdraw when `has_method`, else "Add withdrawal method" (D6). Each Withdraw dialog's Available is what that method reaches (`withdrawReach`: the server's `reach`): Stripe = cleared owed money + money already in its Stripe account; PayPal/Wise = cleared owed money, never the Stripe account's (the old withdrawal pool became ordinary owed money in 20261121350000).
- A refund or dispute after approval never takes money from the balance by itself (20261124100000, payment reviews). While a payment is reviewed, the card adds one line, "Withdrawals are paused while a payment is reviewed. A brand requested a refund for "<step>" on <order>, so our team is reviewing the payment." (a card dispute says so instead, Youssef, Nov 25: "A brand disputed a card payment for "<step>" on <order>, so our team is reviewing it."; Total earnings' help adds "Includes $X disputed." only when some is) (`reviewLines`; the order since 20261124100100, as two orders can share a step's name), Withdraw is off with the first sentence as its hover text, and the Available balance tooltip says "once withdrawals resume" instead of "now" (only while paused). Never say the factory owes anything, did anything wrong, or that "nothing was taken". What staff return to the brand is its own dated row, "Returned to the brand"; a refund or lost dispute before approval keeps its row ("Refunded" / "Reversed by the brand's bank"); the step's row is never rewritten: it stays at what was earned (`stepRowCents`, 20261124100100), so the rows add up to the balance. Available never goes below zero.
- An approved step reads "Added to your balance", never "Pending", unless all its money is in Pending (a card payment still clearing). Withdrawals on their way keep their own rows and words.
- The four tooltips above stay word for word unless Youssef approves a change. Pending was reworded on Nov 20 because it now also holds withdrawals on their way.

Run `npm run check:earnings`, `npm run check:payout-help` and `npm run build` after changing the card or the figures.
